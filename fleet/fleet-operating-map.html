Fleet Operating Map

A small, current reference for how work moves through the fleet. This is architecture, not liveness.

Governance rule. Durable or governed work routes through Paperclip and Ringer. Omnigent may select a harness, but it may not bypass the registered governance layers.

Operator and Omnigent

Interactive work, live gateway, harness selection, and cross-host session brokering. Registered lanes are dispatched through the governance contract.

Paperclip

Projects, work records, approvals, agents, and coordination for every registered execution lane.

↔

Ringer

Manifests, model control, typists, judges, checks, and receipts for every registered execution lane.

Registered execution lanes

Hermes, Claude, Codex, Kimi, Pi, OpenCode, Devin, Cursor, and other verified lanes are sibling lanes under the same governance contract. Each registered lane carries separate lane-contract, adapter, and policy versions.

Canonical evidence and continuity

Paperclip remains the authority record, Ringer emits execution receipts, and Vault retains the durable human-readable mirror. Route decisions carry the lane, capability basis, three independent versions, and no-bypass guard.
